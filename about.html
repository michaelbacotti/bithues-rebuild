About Bithues

A literary publication for curious readers.

Who We Are

Bithues started with a simple frustration: finding a book worth reading felt like wading through noise. Plot summaries everywhere, real analysis almost nowhere. We wanted to create the publication we wished existed — somewhere a curious reader could come and trust that the criticism meant something.

We cover science fiction, fantasy, historical fiction, literary fiction, and the surprising places they overlap. Our reviewers write long reads because some books deserve that kind of attention. We don't chase release dates. We return to books we admire and try to figure out why they work, and we take on books that didn't work and try to understand why not.

Bithues is independent, which means we're free to write what we think rather than what the algorithms reward.

What You'll Find Here

Our reviews are longer than what you'll find elsewhere — not because we're trying to be exhaustive, but because good criticism takes room to breathe. We write about what a book is trying to do, how well it does it, and who it might be for. We're interested in craft: structure, voice, pacing, the way a novel earns its ending.

Beyond reviews, we publish reading guides that go deeper into authors and series worth knowing, and original short fiction from writers working in the margins of familiar genres. We're not interested in content for its own sake — every piece we run should leave you with something to think about, whether that's a new book to find or a fresh way of seeing one you already know.

What We Do

We read widely and write carefully. Our reviews go beyond the plot summary to examine what a book is trying to do, how well it does it, and who it's for. Our original stories explore worlds that linger.

Our Approach

We believe good reading criticism is a form of attention — a way of being present with a book long enough to understand what it actually is. We don't chase release dates or confuse virality for quality. We return to books we admire and try to figure out why they work.

Submitting Work

We accept unsolicited short fiction and are occasionally looking for contributing reviewers. Reach out at our contact page with a brief description of your work and relevant samples.

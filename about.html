About Bithues

Bithues is a home for short fiction — stories that explore what it means to be alive, in worlds familiar, strange, and somewhere in between. We publish original short fiction across dark fantasy, literary fiction, speculative fiction, and the occasional piece that refuses to be categorized.

Every story on Bithues is fiction. Any resemblance to actual events, persons, or places is entirely coincidental — and entirely intentional, where it needs to be.

What you'll find here

Dark fantasy. Literary fiction. Stories that take risks — with voice, with structure, with the way a story moves through the world. We believe short fiction is where writers take their best risks.

We also publish book reviews and articles on reading and books — editorial takes on what we think is worth your time.

Book reviews

Our reviews are honest, independent takes on books we've read and think others should read too. We may earn a small affiliate commission when you purchase through links on this site — at no extra cost to you.

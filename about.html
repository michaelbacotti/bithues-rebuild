About Bithues

Bithues is a home for short fiction — stories that explore what it means to be alive, in worlds familiar, strange, and somewhere in between.

Every story here is fiction. Any resemblance to actual events, persons, or places is entirely coincidental — and entirely intentional, where it needs to be.

What you'll find here

Dark fantasy. Literary fiction. The occasional story that refuses to be categorized. Bithues publishes work that takes risks — with voice, with structure, with the way a story moves through the world.

The author

Writer, reader, collector of strange ideas. Bithues is an independent publication for fiction that deserves a home.

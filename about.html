About Bithues

A literary publication for curious readers.

Bithues is an independent literary publication dedicated to thoughtful book reviews, reading guides, and original fiction. We cover science fiction, fantasy, historical fiction, literary fiction, and the spaces where they overlap.

What We Do

We read widely and write carefully. Our reviews go beyond the plot summary to examine what a book is trying to do, how well it does it, and who it's for. Our original stories explore worlds that linger.

Our Approach

We believe good reading criticism is a form of attention — a way of being present with a book long enough to understand what it actually is. We don't chase release dates or confuse virality for quality. We return to books we admire and try to figure out why they work.

Submitting Work

We accept unsolicited short fiction and are occasionally looking for contributing reviewers. Reach out through our contact page with a brief description of your work and relevant samples.

Bithues is an independent book review site and short fiction publisher. We write honest reviews of books we think are worth your time — fiction, nonfiction, sci-fi, fantasy, and everything in between. We also publish original short fiction.

Who runs Bithues

Bithues is operated by a small, independent team based in the United States. Every book we review is read by a human editor. Every story we publish is read and selected by a human. We are not affiliated with any publisher, literary agency, or large media company.

Meet the Bithues team

Reviews and stories on Bithues are published under pen names, each with a defined coverage area and voice. No single editor pretends to cover everything — the right reviewer is the one who actually reads this kind of book.

Eleanor Ashford

Literary fiction, slow reads

Translated literature, historical fiction, literary romance, and character-driven prehistorical fiction. Reads to inhabit a voice, not to summarize a plot.

Marcus Cole

Sci-fi, fantasy, world-building

Hard SF, space opera, fantasy, horror, and the places where speculative metaphysics meets narrative architecture. Comes from game design, takes craft seriously.

Julian Cross

Cultural criticism, essays

Political and social analysis, UAP and philosophical nonfiction, climate and cultural essays, the publishing industry. Argues with his hands.

Sarah Voss

Short fiction, experimental, debuts

Short story collections, flash fiction, debut novelists, small press, experimental prose, and the literary edges of prehistorical fiction.

David Okonkwo

Non-fiction, guides, practical reads

History, biography, business, thrillers, self-help, travel guides, and our children's books coverage — picture books through early readers and family reads.

Thomas Mercer

Military, survival, action

Military thrillers, survival fiction, action-adventure, international intrigue, geopolitical thrillers, police procedurals, and military history.

Priya Mehta

Literary speculative fiction

Former astrophysicist. Reviews philosophical SF, weird fiction, literary horror, magical realism, slipstream, and contemplative science fiction.

Book reviews

Our reviews are honest, independent takes on books we've read and think others should read. We may earn a small affiliate commission when you purchase through links on this site — at no extra cost to you. Editorial decisions are never influenced by commissions or complimentary copies.

Short fiction

Every story on Bithues is original fiction, published here first. We believe short fiction is where writers take their best risks — with voice, structure, and the way a story moves through the world. We also review books for children and young readers, including the Little Mike series.

About Bithues

Bithues is an independent book review site and short fiction publisher. We write honest reviews of books we think are worth your time — fiction, nonfiction, sci-fi, fantasy, and everything in between. We also publish original short fiction.

Who runs Bithues

Bithues is operated by a small, independent team based in the United States. Every book we review is read by a human editor. Every story we publish is read and selected by a human. We are not affiliated with any publisher, literary agency, or large media company.

Book reviews

Our reviews are honest, independent takes on books we've read and think others should read. We may earn a small affiliate commission when you purchase through links on this site — at no extra cost to you. Editorial decisions are never influenced by commissions or complimentary copies.

Short fiction

Every story on Bithues is original fiction, published here first. We believe short fiction is where writers take their best risks — with voice, structure, and the way a story moves through the world. We also review books for children and young readers, including the Little Mike series.

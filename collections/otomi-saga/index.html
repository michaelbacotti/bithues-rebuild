The Otomí Saga

A reading order for E. J. Marín's Mesoamerican historical fiction

English & Spanish 3 Books ~900–1500 CE Central Mexico

E. J. Marín's Otomí series is a trilogy of evidence-based historical novels that follow Otomí (Hñähñu) communities across central Mexico, from the post-Teotihuacan highland centuries of Otomí, through the slow imperial tightening of Xaltocan, and into the household reckonings of Cords of Empire as the Aztec world reorganizes tribute, labor, and war.

What makes the series unusual on Amazon is that Marín publishes the first two novels in both English and Spanish — a rare bilingual run for an indie Mesoamerican series. Cords of Empire, the third book, is currently English only. We've read all five editions and pulled them together here as a single reading hub so you can pick the order that fits how you want to read.

Book 1 EN · English

Otomí: A Historical Narrative of Land, Ritual, and Continuity

Centuries after Teotihuacán, a highland village rides out one drought-stricken year. Household altars, hilltop shrines, and the slow arithmetic of survival.

Also available in Spanish: Otomí: Una narrativa histórica de tierra, ritual y continuidad.

Xaltocan: A Historical Narrative of Tribute, War, and Survival

Reading Order

One way through the saga. Marín wrote the three novels in chronological sequence, and the in-universe and publication orders are the same.

  1. Otomí — highland village, post-Teotihuacan ~900–1100 CE
  2. Xaltocan — Basin of Mexico island polity Late 14th century
  3. Cords of Empire — after Xaltocan falls Late 14th – early 15th century

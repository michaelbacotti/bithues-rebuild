Bithues is an independent book review site and short fiction publisher, run by a small team based in the United States. We don’t have a press office, a submissions portal, or a ticketing system. We have one editor and one inbox — and we read everything that comes in.

If you’re not sure where your message belongs, info@bithues.com is always the right place to start. We’ll route it from there.

How can we help?

Most messages fall into one of the four channels below. Use the address that fits — it helps us reply faster and keeps the right thing in front of the right person.

Story submissions

submissions@bithues.com

Send us your short fiction — 1,000 to 12,000 words. Original work only; simultaneous submissions are fine if you let us know. Paste the story in the body of the email, or attach a clean .doc or .docx. Include a short bio and a one-sentence pitch. We respond to every submission within four weeks.

Book review requests

reviews@bithues.com

Authors, publicists, and publishers: send a one-paragraph pitch, a press kit or sample chapter, and the publication date. We do not guarantee a review, and we do not accept payment, gifts, or affiliate placement in exchange for coverage. We read what we’re sent and we tell you honestly whether it’s a fit.

Press, partnerships & rights

press@bithues.com

Interview requests, podcast appearances, anthology invitations, translation and reprint rights, syndication, and brand or partnership inquiries. Tell us what you have in mind, the timeline, and any constraints (exclusivity, embargoes, deadlines).

Everything else

info@bithues.com

Reader letters, corrections, accessibility questions, takedown requests, and notes from people who just want to say hello. This is also the right address for privacy and data questions — we respond to every one.

What to include

A few details make it easier for us to act on a message quickly:

  • Your name and how you’d like to be addressed in any reply.
  • A subject line that names the topic — “Submission: The Quiet Town” beats “Hello!” every time.
  • Links, not attachments — for press kits, author sites, retailer pages, and excerpts. We’ll ask for the full file if we need it.
  • Your timezone — only if the timing of a reply actually matters.

What we won’t do

In the interest of being clear about who we are and what we do:

  • We don’t run paid reviews, sponsored posts, or “advertorial” content, and we don’t accept compensation in exchange for coverage.
  • We don’t sell, rent, or trade reader email addresses. The newsletter is opt-in only and you can unsubscribe in one click.
  • We don’t respond to AI-generated outreach, link-swap requests, or “quick favor” SEO pitches. Those go straight to the archive.

How long does a reply take?

Submissions: within four weeks, usually sooner. If you haven’t heard from us in six, feel free to follow up — we won’t have forgotten you, but we may have missed the message.

Review requests and press: within five business days. We’ll tell you yes, no, or “not right now” — and we’ll mean it.

Reader letters and corrections: within a few days. Faster if it’s a correction; we’d rather fix something today than next week.

Snail mail

For rights, contracts, and legal correspondence, a physical mailing address is available on request — please email press@bithues.com and we’ll send it. We don’t publish a street address on the open web.

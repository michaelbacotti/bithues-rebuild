What is Bithues?

Bithues is an independent book review site and indie short-fiction publisher. We publish honest, editor-vetted reviews of books we think are worth your time — from sci-fi, fantasy, and literary fiction to nonfiction, children’s literature, and everything in between. We also publish original short stories, written and curated by an independent editorial team you can name.

“A luminous, quietly breathtaking first-contact novel that feels as intimate as a family journal and as vast as the night sky it invites us to look up at.”

— Priya Mehta, reviewing First Contact Diary by Mira Ellison

Below you’ll find our latest articles, short stories, and book reviews. Use the navigation above to browse by section, or visit the About page to learn more about who we are and how we work.

How Bithues works

Bithues is run by a small editorial team of working writers and editors. Each review you read here was written by the editor whose name is on the masthead, after they read the book end to end. The reviews are not paid for, are not contingent on the publisher providing the book, and are not influenced by any party other than the editor reading the book. Affiliate links, where they appear, are tied to the specific Bithues recommendation, not to a paid placement.

The short fiction on the site is original work published with the writers’ permission under a non-exclusive license. We publish new fiction weekly, with a weekly roundup on Sunday afternoons. The fiction desk and the reviews desk are separate functions; the editorial team reads both, but the work is split so neither function is degraded by the other’s deadlines.

What you’ll find on the homepage

The editor’s picks at the top of the page are updated when a new review, article, or short story warrants a featured slot. Featured here means the editorial team thinks the piece is worth your time and wants to make sure you see it — it does not mean the piece is more important than any other piece on the site. The “more articles,” “more book reviews,” and “more stories” lists below the editor’s picks are the recent pieces in each category, sorted by publication date with the most recent first.

How new reviews are written

A new review takes one to two weeks from outline to publication. The review’s editor reads the book in full, writes a draft that frames the book’s argument or its lack of one, has a second editor read the draft for tone and accuracy, then publishes. Reviews are not first impressions; the books have been read end to end before the review is written, and the review takes its time before asking the reader to take theirs.

How we choose what to review

We choose what to review based on what the editorial team is reading. Books come to us in three ways: the editor picks the book up because they want to read it; the publisher sends us a book that they think will fit our editorial coverage; or we hear about a book from a reader whose recommendation the editor takes seriously. The books we accept for review are books we intend to read in full and engage with on their own terms, and we are honest with publishers when a book is not a fit for our coverage.

How to support Bithues

Bithues is supported by advertising and by an optional paid newsletter subscription. The advertising does not influence the editorial content, and the editorial content is the same whether or not you are a paid subscriber. Readers who want to support the site beyond the free coverage can subscribe to the paid tier; readers who want the free editorial coverage can simply read. The full revenue model is documented on the About page and is revisited annually.

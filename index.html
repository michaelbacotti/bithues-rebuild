What is Bithues?

Bithues is an independent book review site and indie short-fiction publisher. We publish honest, editor-vetted reviews of books we think are worth your time — from sci-fi, fantasy, and literary fiction to nonfiction, children’s literature, and everything in between. We also publish original short stories, written and curated by an independent editorial team you can name.

What is Bithues?

Bithues is an independent book review site and indie short-fiction publisher. We publish honest, editor-vetted reviews of books we think are worth your time — from sci-fi, fantasy, and literary fiction to nonfiction, children’s literature, and everything in between. We also publish original short stories, written and curated by an independent editorial team you can name.

What this site covers

The site covers four overlapping areas of reading: book reviews (long-form reviews of specific titles across literary fiction, science fiction, fantasy, historical fiction, nonfiction, and children's books), articles (essays and analysis on reading, the publishing industry, and the nature of fiction), lists (curated reading lists organized by genre, mood, and reader profile), and shop (a small selection of products inspired by the site's reading material). All four sections are produced by the same editorial team and follow the same standards documented on the About page.

The site is intended for readers who want a thoughtful, substantive voice on books. We are not a news outlet, a publisher, or a retailer in the traditional sense; we are an editorial publication that occasionally recommends or sells products related to the books we cover. The site is reader-supported through advertising, an optional paid newsletter, and a small affiliate program.

How to use the site

The best starting point depends on what you are looking for. Readers looking for a specific book can use the search function or browse the reviews by genre. Readers looking for ideas about what to read next can start with the lists, which are organized by genre and reader profile. Readers interested in the broader topics of reading and the publishing industry can start with the articles section. Readers looking for products can visit the shop.

The site is updated regularly — typically with one or two new pieces per week, plus the daily newsletter for subscribers. The site map and recent updates are linked in the footer. Readers who want to follow along can subscribe to the newsletter, follow the RSS feed, or check back periodically.

Editorial standards

Every published piece goes through a three-step review process: drafting by the assigned editor, technical or editorial review by a second desk member, and a final pass by the editor-in-chief before publication. We do not publish pieces we have not fact-checked against primary sources where primary sources exist. Where a claim is made about a specific book (publication date, publisher, page count, plot summary), the source is cited inline. Where a claim is interpretive (the book's argument, its place in the genre), the reasoning is stated explicitly and the limitations of the interpretation are named.

Corrections are appended to the bottom of the affected page with a dated correction note. Minor corrections (typos, broken links, formatting) are made silently and noted in the next published post. The correction policy is documented on the About page.

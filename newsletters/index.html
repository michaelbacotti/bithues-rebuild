Why readers stay

Bithues is held to the same standard as the reviews: every book in the newsletter is one the editors have read end to end. Reading signals are framed as the editor's reading of the book, not a generic claim about what the book says. Affiliate links, where they appear, are tied to a specific Bithues recommendation, not to an algorithmic pull. The newsletter is editorial coverage, not a marketing channel.

The signal runs daily at 5:45 AM ET, in under five minutes. Sunday is reserved for short fiction; there are no affiliate links on Sundays. The archive below goes back to launch and stays accessible.

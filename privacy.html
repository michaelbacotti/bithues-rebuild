Privacy Policy

What we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Last updated: May 2026

Information We Collect

Bithues collects minimal information. When you visit this site, our hosting provider and any analytics tools we use may record standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and the time of your visit. This data is used in aggregate form only — to understand how the site is being used, not to identify individual visitors.

We do not require you to create an account, subscribe to a newsletter, or submit any personal information to read our content.

Cookies

This site uses cookies to enable functionality and to understand aggregate usage patterns. The cookies used fall into the following categories:

  • Essential cookies: Required for site navigation and basic functionality. These cannot be disabled without affecting your experience.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors move through the site and where they come from. We use this data to improve content and structure.
  • Advertising cookies: Used by our advertising partners to deliver relevant advertisements based on your interests. These cookies are set by third parties and are governed by the privacy policies of those third parties.

Google AdSense and Advertising

We display advertisements through Google AdSense. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of Google's use of cookies by visiting Google's Ads Settings or by visiting the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your interests and to track conversions. This means Google may show you ads on other sites based on what it knows about your browsing history. To learn more about how Google uses your data in the advertising context, please review the Google Privacy & Terms.

Bithues does not sell or share personal information with advertisers. All data collected through our advertising partners is governed by their respective privacy policies.

Third-Party Links

Some pages on this site include links to external retailers (such as Amazon). If you click through and make a purchase, those retailers may collect data under their own privacy policies. Bithues is not responsible for the data practices of any external site. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any retailer you visit.

Affiliate Links

Bithues participates in the Amazon Associates Program and may earn commissions from qualifying purchases made through links on this site. This does not affect the price you pay. Recommendations made on Bithues are editorial and are not influenced by any affiliate relationship.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at hello@bithues.com and we will take reasonable steps to remove that data.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information collected on this site. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure. We cannot guarantee the security of your data, but we do our best to make it reasonably safe.

Your Rights (GDPR and CCPA)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or a jurisdiction that recognizes similar rights, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. You may also have the right to object to or restrict certain processing, and the right to data portability.

If you are a California resident under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you have the right to know what data we collect, request deletion of your data, and opt out of the sale of your personal information. As noted above, Bithues does not sell your personal information.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at hello@bithues.com. We will respond to your request within the timeframe required by applicable law.

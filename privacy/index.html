What this policy covers

This privacy policy describes what information is collected when you visit this site, how that information is used, and the choices you have regarding the information. The policy applies to all pages on this domain. The policy is incorporated by reference into any service offered on the site (newsletter subscriptions, contact form submissions, comment threads, and any other interactive features).

Information collected automatically

When you visit the site, standard technical information is collected through server logs and analytics tools. This includes your IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referring URL, pages visited, time spent on each page, and approximate geographic location (country and city-level, not precise). The information is collected through server logs (retained for 90 days) and through Google Analytics, a third-party analytics service governed by Google's privacy policy.

We use this information to understand how visitors use the site, to identify and fix technical problems, and to improve the content over time. We do not combine this information with personally identifiable information. We do not sell or share this information with third parties for their own purposes.

Cookies and similar technologies

The site uses cookies for two purposes. The first is functional: cookies remember your preferences (theme settings, font size, last-read article) so the site can present the appropriate version on return visits. These cookies do not contain personal information and do not track you across other sites. The second is advertising: Google AdSense uses cookies to serve ads that are relevant to your interests and to measure ad performance. You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ad Settings page.

You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences. Disabling cookies entirely may affect the functionality of the site (preferences may not be remembered) but will not prevent you from accessing the content.

Your rights and choices

You have the right to request a copy of any personal information we hold about you; to request that we correct or delete your personal information; to opt out of marketing emails (via the unsubscribe link in any email or by contacting us directly); and to lodge a complaint with the appropriate data protection authority in your jurisdiction. To exercise any of these rights, email the contact listed in the site footer. We respond to verified requests within 30 days.

Children's privacy

The site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected personal information from a child under 13, please contact us so we can delete the information.

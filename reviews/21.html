The Reviews section offers deeper looks at books that merit more than a star rating. Each review explores what makes a book worth your time, who should read it, and what it offers that you will not find elsewhere.

What You'll Find Here

Fiction Reviews

Novels across genres — literary fiction, science fiction, fantasy, thrillers, and the spaces where genres blur. These reviews focus on what a book is trying to do and how well it does it, rather than forcing everything into a single standard of excellence.

Nonfiction Reviews

Memoirs, histories, science, and essays. The nonfiction reviews are interested in both argument and style — a book that is right but boring still has a problem, and we'll say so.

Classic Revisits

Older books that are often referenced but rarely read. These reviews try to answer the question: is this book still worth your time, or has it been replaced by better work that came after?

Guilty Pleasures

Every serious reader has them. These reviews take them seriously anyway.

How We Review

We rate books on three dimensions:

Craft — How well is this book written? Is the prose doing something interesting? Does the structure serve the story?

Impact — Did this book change how you think? Did it stay with you after you finished? Does it still matter?

Recommendability — Would you recommend this to a specific person, or only to a specific type of reader?

A book that scores highly on all three is rare. Most good books score well on one or two. We tell you which.

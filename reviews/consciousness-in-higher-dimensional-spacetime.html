What if consciousness doesn't exist in the same spacetime as the body? A radical extension of Einstein's relativity into the domain of mind.

Consciousness in Higher Dimensional Spacetime continues Quantum Chronos's project of examining what consciousness is and where it exists. Where The Physics of Time argued that consciousness actualizes moments within the block universe, this volume asks an even more radical question: what if consciousness itself occupies a different dimensional structure than the physical body?

Chronos draws on higher-dimensional physics—the kind of mathematics that underlies string theory and M-theory—to propose that consciousness may not be localizable to three-dimensional space at all. Instead, it may be a pattern that exists across additional dimensions of spacetime, only partially visible from the familiar four-dimensional slice we call reality.

This is ambitious, speculative territory. Chronos is careful to label what is established physics, what is theoretical extension, and what is speculation—a methodological rigor that distinguishes this work from more credulous popular treatments of consciousness and physics. The writing is dense but rewarding for readers willing to follow the argument.

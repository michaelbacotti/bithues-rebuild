Book Review January 2026

Shadow Work Journal for Women

by Luna Sage

Self-Help

Shadow Work Journal for Women by Luna Sage offers a guided 90-day program in shadow work — the psychological practice of examining and integrating the parts of oneself that have been denied, suppressed, or hidden. The book is designed specifically for readers who are new to this territory and want a structured, supportive entry point.

The journal format is the right choice for this material. Shadow work is inherently reflective, and a book that asks readers to write through difficult emotional territory needs to provide both the prompts and the space. Sage's prompts are warm without being saccharine, specific without being prescriptive.

The 90-day structure provides enough duration to build genuine habit without being so long that readers lose momentum. Each week has a loose thematic focus — childhood patterns, relationship dynamics, professional identity — while leaving room for individual variation in what each reader needs to work through.

This is not a book for everyone. Readers looking for quick fixes or surface-level positivity will be disappointed. Readers ready to do genuine inner work will find a reliable and thoughtful companion in these pages.

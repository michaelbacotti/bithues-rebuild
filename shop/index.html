A print shop in the wildwoods — cosmic, cottagecore, curious.

Welcome to the Wildwood Press Shop, the print-on-demand sister studio of Bithues. Every design here is born from a love of quiet science, cosmic wonder, and the kind of curious collectibles that earn a place on a shelf instead of buried in a drawer. We make tees, mugs, and canvas prints for the readers, tinkerers, and stargazers among us — designed for the friend who has a telescope and a coffee table that needs anchoring.

All items are printed on demand through Printify and fulfilled by US-based partners. Shopping + checkout happens on our Etsy storefront at WildwoodPressShop — every product page here includes a direct link to add the item to your Etsy cart.

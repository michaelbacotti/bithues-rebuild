About this design

Jupiter, the most massive planet in our solar system, wrapped around your morning coffee. This ceramic mug puts the swirling cloud bands of the gas giant right at your fingertips — gold, amber, and cream striations across the surface, the iconic Great Red Spot churning at the center, the sheer impossible scale of it all rendered in vivid detail. Every sip is a reminder that you're sharing a universe with something this magnificent. Why this design works: The full-wrap Jupiter composition turns the mug into a small portable window onto planetary science. The warm sepia-and-gold palette pairs beautifully with morning coffee (which is, conveniently, also warm). The Great Red Spot becomes a small meditation on permanence — that storm has been raging for at least 350 years. Perfect for: planetary scientists, amateur astronomers, anyone with a telescope and a favorite gas giant, the friend who thinks Saturn gets too much attention, and anyone who believes Jupiter has the best rings nobody ever talks about. Product details: 11oz and 15oz options. High-quality sublimation printing on white ceramic, microwave and dishwasher safe. Printed on demand, US shipping 2–5 business days.

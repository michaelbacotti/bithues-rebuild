About this design

The view that changed everything. A ceramic mug featuring the moon rising over the curve of Earth, the dark side of the lunar surface in stark contrast against the bright blue limb of our home planet. The kind of image that astronauts say stays with them forever — and now you can hold it in your hand every morning while the coffee does its own small version of the same thing. Why this design works: The full-wrap composition puts the lunar horizon right at the rim of the mug, so when you look down into your coffee, you're looking down at the moon. It's a small visual trick that makes the mug feel less like a vessel and more like a window. Perfect for: astronauts (aspiring and otherwise), moon lovers, astronomy teachers, anyone who has ever stopped a conversation to look at the moon. A great gift for the friend who has strong opinions about lunar phases. Product details: 11oz and 15oz options. High-quality sublimation printing on white ceramic, microwave and dishwasher safe.
